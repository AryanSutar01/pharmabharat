Subscription funds the business. Predictable, defensible, and it is what pharmacies already pay rivals for worse software.

Take rate scales the business. It grows with the shop’s purchasing without us adding headcount.

Streams 3 to 5 are only possible because of batch data nobody else is collecting. That is the moat earning revenue.

Pricing is a model assumption benchmarked against incumbent pharmacy software, not a tested price point. Stream 5 runs on explicit shop consent.

The order matters: we do not need streams three to five to be a business, but they are where the margin is, and only our data makes them possible. If asked about data privacy on stream five, be firm — anonymised, aggregated, opt-in, and the shop can withdraw.
