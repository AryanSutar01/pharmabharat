Priced from the strip’s MRP, decremented exactly, and the open strip stays in inventory with its batch and expiry intact. Every competitor forces a whole-pack sale or an untracked manual adjustment.

First to expire, first offered

The counter searches the shop’s own shelf and always offers the batch that dies soonest — not the newest, not the one on top.

That single default solves most of the expiry problem quietly, every time anybody sells anything.

Plus: GST bill with batch and expiry printed, in about fifteen seconds.

Loose dispensing is not an edge case in India — it is the majority of counter transactions by volume.

This is the slide that convinces anyone who has stood behind a pharmacy counter. Do not rush the loose-tablet sequence. The strategic point: this is a small feature that no incumbent can retrofit cheaply, because it changes the unit of inventory itself.
