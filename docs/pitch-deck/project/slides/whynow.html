Why now

Four things became true in the same decade

GST made the data structured

Every B2B pharma transaction now has a machine-readable tax identity behind it. Ten years ago there was nothing to verify a trading partner against.

The counter already has a smartphone

Cheap data and UPI put a connected device on every pharmacy counter. The hardware barrier that killed earlier attempts is gone.

Regulation is tightening on traceability

Barcoding mandates on top drug brands and growing pressure on recall accountability push the whole chain toward batch-level records.

Open networks arrived

ONDC and ABDM give a small platform national distribution and clinical interoperability it could never have built alone.

The expensive part was never the idea. It was that until recently nobody could get the batch into a system cheaply enough for a shop earning thin margins to bother.

Investors ask “why hasn’t this been done?” This slide is the answer: it could not have been, affordably, until these four lined up. Lead with GST — it is the least obvious and the most convincing.
