Honest position

SWOT

Strengths

  • Batch and expiry modelled as first-class data — structurally, not as a report
  • A complete counter system, not a buying app bolted onto a shop
  • Unit-level dispensing: we can sell four tablets; most rivals cannot
  • Built and verified — 41/41 requirements, 208 automated tests
  • Three languages at the counter, and a very low cost base

Weaknesses

  • No paying pharmacy yet, and no counter has run a full day on it
  • A student team, part-time until the first funding
  • No field sales experience — the hardest part of this market
  • GST treatment still awaiting a chartered accountant’s sign-off
  • Infrastructure proven for a pilot, not yet load-tested at scale

Opportunities

  • No incumbent owns batch-level data across both buying and selling
  • ONDC and ABDM offer national reach without national spend
  • Near-expiry redistribution is an untouched market with real money in it
  • Ledger data makes credit underwritable where collateral never was
  • The same core extends to agro-chemical and veterinary distribution

Threats

  • Entrenched billing software with decades of installed base and habit
  • Funded health-commerce players moving down into B2B distribution
  • Distributors resisting price transparency they currently benefit from
  • Regulatory change in traceability or e-pharmacy rules
  • A long sales cycle: pharmacists change software rarely and reluctantly

The weaknesses column is the one we are asking for help with. The strengths column is the one that is already finished.

Do not soften the weaknesses. An investor has seen a hundred SWOTs where the weaknesses are disguised strengths; a real one buys credibility for everything else. If asked which worries us most, say field sales — and mean it.
