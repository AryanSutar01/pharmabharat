Why our AI is defensible

Anyone can buy the model. Nobody else has the data.

More shops bill

on the platform, every day

Batch events accrue

per tablet, with a date attached

Forecasts sharpen

expiry, demand, substitution

Shops lose less

measured in rupees, monthly

Leaving costs money

so more shops join

The dataset does not exist anywhere else

Billing software holds sales without batches. Distributors hold batches without sales. We are the only point where a batch number, an expiry date, a purchase price and a dispensing event meet on the same row.

Built so the data can be trusted

Because stock moves inside database transactions, our training data cannot drift from what physically happened on the shelf. Most retail datasets in India cannot make that claim.

Customer data stays the shop’s. Cross-shop models are consented, anonymised and — from Horizon 2 — federated, so raw records never leave the premises.

This is the slide that separates us from “we will add AI”. The argument is not that our models are better; it is that the training data is structurally unavailable to anyone else, and transactionally honest. Expect the privacy question — the footer is the answer.
