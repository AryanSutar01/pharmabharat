Target by year 3

₹15 cr

5,000 pharmacies at ₹30,000 blended — 2% of the serviceable market, reachable from ten district clusters.

The blended figure is not a subscription guess. It is ₹12,000 of software, roughly ₹10,000 of marketplace take on the share of purchasing we route, and the balance from redistribution and credit facilitation.

A bottom-up model, not a measured market. Pharmacy-count and revenue-per-shop assumptions are stated so they can be challenged.

Never present this as a researched market size. It is a bottom-up model and saying so earns more credibility than a big borrowed number. If challenged on ₹30,000, walk through the three components — it holds up.
