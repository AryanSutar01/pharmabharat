Future scope · the AI roadmap

Fifteen models. One dataset. Three horizons.

Horizon 1 · 0–12 months

Intelligence layer

  • Predictive Expiry Engine — a per-batch survival model forecasting, 60 days out, which stock will die before it sells
  • Demand Forecasting — molecule and shop level, aware of season, festival and local epidemic signal
  • Autonomous Replenishment — drafts the order, optimises the split, waits for one tap
  • Vision Batch Capture — photograph an invoice or a strip; batch, expiry and HSN enter with no typing
  • Substitution Intelligence — generics ranked by margin, stock and patient saving at the moment of billing

Horizon 2 · 1–2 years

Network effects

  • National Near-Expiry Exchange — a live market routing stock from where it will expire to where it will sell
  • Embedded Working Capital — credit underwritten from ledger behaviour, not collateral
  • Voice-First Counter — hands-free dispensing in Hindi, Marathi and eight further Indian languages
  • ABDM & ONDC — e-prescriptions and open-network commerce at national scale
  • Federated Learning — models trained across thousands of shops without a shop’s data ever leaving it

Horizon 3 · 2–5 years

Category-defining

  • Spurious-Drug Detection — graph anomaly detection over batch provenance to surface counterfeit flows
  • Blockchain Provenance — immutable manufacturer-to-patient chain of custody
  • Cold-Chain Digital Twin — live IoT temperature telemetry bound to the batch record itself
  • Clinical Adherence Engine — chronic-patient refill prediction and timely intervention
  • Pharmaco-Signal Grid — anonymised real-time dispensing as public-health early warning

Every model above trains on data the platform already captures. The hard part — getting every batch and every date into a system at all — is built.

Do not read fifteen bullets. Name one per horizon and land the closing line. The credibility of this slide comes entirely from the previous one: we are not promising to collect the data, we are promising to use data the system already produces.
