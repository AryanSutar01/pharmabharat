The wedge

The expiry date, end to end, with no manual step in it

Captured

when the batch arrives, from the order it came on

Watched

at 90, 60 and 30 days — once per batch, never twice

Respected

at the counter, where the oldest batch is offered first

Counted

in rupees, at cost, on the report the owner reads

A shop that can see this number can manage it. Today, almost no shop in India can see it at all.

This is the accent slide and the emotional centre of the deck. Four words, one chain. The last line is the pitch: we are not selling software, we are selling a number the owner has never had.
