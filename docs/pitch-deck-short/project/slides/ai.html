Future scope · AI

Anyone can buy the model. Nobody else has the data.

Horizon 1 · 0–12 months

Intelligence layer

  • Predictive Expiry Engine — forecasts 60 days out which batch dies before it sells
  • Demand Forecasting — by molecule and shop, aware of season and local epidemic signal
  • Autonomous Replenishment — drafts the order, optimises the split, waits for one tap
  • Vision Batch Capture — photograph an invoice; batch, expiry and HSN enter untyped

Horizon 2 · 1–2 years

Network effects

  • National Near-Expiry Exchange — routes stock from where it expires to where it sells
  • Embedded Working Capital — credit from ledger behaviour, not collateral
  • Voice-First Counter — hands-free dispensing in ten Indian languages
  • Federated Learning — models trained across shops without data leaving them

Horizon 3 · 2–5 years

Category-defining

  • Spurious-Drug Detection — graph anomaly detection over batch provenance
  • Cold-Chain Digital Twin — live IoT telemetry bound to the batch record
  • Clinical Adherence Engine — chronic-patient refill prediction and intervention
  • Pharmaco-Signal Grid — anonymised dispensing as public-health early warning

The dataset exists nowhere else. Billing software holds sales without batches; distributors hold batches without sales. We are the only point where a batch number, an expiry date, a purchase price and a dispensing event meet on one row.

And it can be trusted. Stock moves inside database transactions, so training data cannot drift from what physically happened on the shelf. Few retail datasets in India can claim that.

Every model trains on data the platform already captures. The hard part — getting every batch and every date into a system at all — is built.

Do not read twelve bullets. Name one per horizon, then spend the time on the two bands underneath — they are the actual argument. Expect a privacy question: consented, anonymised, and federated from Horizon 2.
