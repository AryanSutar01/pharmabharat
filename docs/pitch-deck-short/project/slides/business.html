Subscription funds the business and take rate scales it. Streams three to five are possible only because of batch data nobody else collects — that is the moat earning revenue.

A bottom-up model, not a measured market. Pricing is benchmarked against incumbent pharmacy software; CAC and payback are assumptions, not results.

Say “bottom-up model” before anyone asks. If challenged on ₹30,000, break it down: ₹12,000 software, about ₹10,000 marketplace take, the rest redistribution and credit. It holds.
