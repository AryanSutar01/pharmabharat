Stock is held in tablets, not strips. The open strip keeps its batch and expiry. Software that cannot cut a strip does not get used in an Indian pharmacy — and loose dispensing is the majority of counter sales by volume.

The counter always offers the batch that expires first. That one default solves most of the expiry problem, quietly, every time anyone sells anything.
