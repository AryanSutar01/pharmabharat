Honest position

SWOT

Strengths

  • Batch and expiry modelled structurally, not as a report
  • A complete counter, not a buying app bolted onto a shop
  • Unit-level dispensing — we can sell four tablets
  • Built and verified: 41/41 requirements, 208 tests

Weaknesses

  • No paying pharmacy, and no counter has run a full day on it
  • A student team, part-time until first funding
  • No field sales experience — the hardest part of this market
  • GST treatment still awaiting a chartered accountant’s sign-off

Opportunities

  • No incumbent owns batch data across both buying and selling
  • ONDC and ABDM give national reach without national spend
  • Near-expiry redistribution is untouched, with real money in it
  • Ledger data makes credit underwritable where collateral never was

Threats

  • Entrenched billing software with decades of habit behind it
  • Funded health-commerce players moving down into B2B
  • Distributors resisting price transparency they benefit from
  • A long sales cycle — pharmacists change software reluctantly

The weaknesses column is what we are asking for help with. The strengths column is already finished.

Do not soften the weaknesses — a real one buys credibility for everything else on the slide. Asked which worries us most: field sales, and mean it.
